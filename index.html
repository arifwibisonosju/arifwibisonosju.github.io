About Me

I am a researcher specializing in Computational Modeling and Intelligent Systems, with a focus on AI-driven simulation, mathematical modeling, and computational optimization. My research explores the integration of artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, and physics-based modeling to understand, simulate, and solve complex real-world problems.

With a Ph.D. in Intelligent Mechatronics Engineering, my research experience includes the modeling, simulation, and development of autonomous and cooperative systems, particularly in dynamic and uncertain environments. My work combines physics-based and data-driven approaches to develop intelligent computational models, simulation frameworks, and adaptive decision-making systems.

My current research interests include:
• Computational and mathematical modeling
• Intelligent systems and artificial intelligence
• AI-driven simulation and computational optimization
• Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MADDPG, MAPPO, MODDPG)
• Autonomous and cooperative systems
• Digital twin and cyber-physical systems
• Simulation-based analysis and decision-making under uncertainty

I am open to interdisciplinary research collaborations in computational modeling, artificial intelligence, simulation, and optimization, particularly for addressing complex and open-ended real-world problems.
